# Pip and the Missing Picnic Basket — narration and shot plan

Target audience: ages 4–8  
Target duration: 52 seconds  
Style: colourful 3D preschool mystery, warm and playful

| Time | Narration | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Music introduction | The mystery title pops up over the sunny orchard. |
| 3–10s | “Hello, friends! I packed a picnic, but my basket has vanished. Will you help me find it?” | Pip bounces in, looks around, and asks the audience for help. |
| 10–17s | “A clue! Tiny footprints. Hop, hop, hop! They lead toward the old apple tree.” | The first clue card appears as Pip follows the trail. |
| 17–24s | “And look—three little crumbs. Count with me: one, two, three. The trail goes this way!” | Three crumbs pop up one by one and Pip counts them. |
| 24–31s | “Now the trail wiggles left, then right, then disappears! Oh, where could the basket be?” | Pip searches in both directions before stopping, puzzled. |
| 31–38s | “Surprise! Bunny has the basket. He was preparing a picnic for everyone.” | Bunny and the fruit-filled basket appear. |
| 38–45s | Bunny: “I should have asked first, Pip.” Pip: “That’s okay. Next time, please ask.” | Bunny apologises and Pip responds kindly. |
| 45–52s | “Friends share, friends ask, and picnics are sweeter together!” | Pip and Bunny bounce beside the picnic basket as the closing message appears. |

## Story note

The mystery, counting moment and call-and-response lines invite children to participate.
The ending models asking permission, apologising and sharing without becoming a lecture.
