# Tiny Tales varied-story roadmap

Updated: 2026-08-24

This roadmap records user-requested concepts for future production. Before rendering one, compare it with `COVERED-TOPICS.md`, live queued metadata, and the latest five completed concepts. Each production must retain the distinct format, movement system, visual language, interaction, and voice noted below; do not reduce these ideas to one reusable quiz template.

## Priority production order

1. **Maya and the Rainy-Day Joey Rescue**
   - User direction: children helping an animal.
   - Story: Maya notices a young kangaroo separated from its family near a rain shelter. She stays calm, keeps a safe distance, asks a trusted ranger for help, and prepares a warm, quiet waiting space. The reunion is the payoff.
   - Structure: child-led cause-and-effect rescue story; no collecting and no multiple-choice cards.
   - Movement/visual system: Maya walks, kneels, points, carries a folded towel, and signals the ranger; the joey peeks, shivers, settles, and hops toward its family. Illustrated rainy park changes gradually from storm to sunset.
   - Participation: practise a quiet voice, point out safe shelter items, and copy a gentle “give space” hand signal.
   - Voice: `natasha-au` narrator, with `maisie-uk` for Maya if dialogue is used.

2. **Star Friends’ Twinkle Playground**
   - User direction: “Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star” with children playing with star-like characters.
   - Story/song: children meet expressive star characters who bounce light between a cloud trampoline, moon seesaw, comet slide, and constellation skipping path.
   - Structure: public-domain melody with an original Tiny Tales arrangement, new connecting verses, movement breaks, and a calm bedtime landing.
   - Movement/visual system: ensemble playground choreography and light trails; no quest tokens.
   - Voice: `ana-us` lead with a distinct child-friendly chorus treatment.

3. **Niko’s Ninja Soccer Kindness Match**
   - User direction: kid ninja soccer player.
   - Story: Niko uses quiet focus, balance, passing, and teamwork—not fighting—to help a nervous new teammate join a playful match.
   - Structure: sports story with skill demonstrations, freeze poses, and a teamwork finale.
   - Movement/visual system: ground-level pitch tracking, ball arcs, footwork trails, goal celebration; bold paper-cut sports graphics.
   - Voice: `ryan-uk` narrator and `ana-us` for Niko’s short dialogue.

4. **Pippa Paintbrush Wakes the Town**
   - User direction: paintbrush song.
   - Story/song: Pippa is a lively paintbrush character who learns that colours can mix, patterns can repeat, and every child’s marks can look different.
   - Structure: original call-and-response song with painting actions rather than a location quest.
   - Movement/visual system: brush sweeps, paint splashes, colour mixing, stamping and spirals on an expanding canvas.
   - Voice: `maisie-uk`; original music and lyrics only.
   - First distinct treatment completed as `Brio's Paintbrush Colour Workshop`: Brio restores a blank canvas through red/yellow/blue air-painting, orange/green mixing, a canvas-meadow transformation and a gallery finale. It uses `ryan-uk`, ten participation gaps and original rhythmic music. Preserve Pippa's town-waking version as a possible later story only if it is redesigned rather than duplicated.

5. **The Ant and the Grasshopper’s Shared Harvest**
   - User direction: ant and grasshopper song.
   - Story/song: an original musical retelling inspired by the public-domain Aesop fable. Ant models planning, Grasshopper contributes music and later helps gather; both learn preparation and kindness without shaming play.
   - Structure: alternating work/play rhythms that join in the finale.
   - Movement/visual system: side-scrolling meadow, changing seasons, seed-carrying choreography, instrument patterns.
   - Voice: `natasha-au` narrator, `ryan-uk` Grasshopper, `ana-us` Ant.

6. **Five Little Ducks’ River-Day Detour**
   - User direction: five little ducks song.
   - Story/song: a gentle traditional-song adaptation in which each duck pauses at a different safe river discovery before everyone returns.
   - Structure: counting-down song with distinct mini-scenes and a counting-up reunion.
   - Movement/visual system: swimming formations, ripples, reeds, bridge reflections and homeward wake trails.
   - Voice: `maisie-uk`; original arrangement and sound design.

7. **Peek-a-Boo House of Surprises**
   - User direction: peek-a-boo song.
   - Story/song: an original song follows children and friendly object characters hiding behind curtains, cushions, cardboard doors and garden leaves.
   - Structure: spatial-language play using behind, under, beside and inside; no animal-card quiz.
   - Movement/visual system: theatrical reveal panels, camera pans, fabric movement and child gestures.
   - Voice: `ana-us`, with varied character responses.

8. **Tiny Tales Shark Family Reef Dance**
   - User direction: baby shark song.
   - Story/song: use the traditional shark-family premise only, with an original Tiny Tales arrangement, original verses, original choreography and a reef-care story.
   - Structure: family dance plus a gentle reef clean-up; do not imitate Pinkfong audio, timing, character designs, branding or video composition.
   - Movement/visual system: underwater dance formations, bubble rhythms, coral pathways and clean-up teamwork.
   - Voice: `ryan-uk` lead with a separate family chorus treatment.

9. **Everyone’s Different Birthday Parade**
   - User direction: birthday celebration.
   - Story/song: children prepare a celebration that accommodates different favourite foods, sounds, family traditions and comfort levels.
   - Structure: original birthday song and preparation story; the child chooses loud, soft or quiet ways to celebrate.
   - Movement/visual system: invitation collage, decoration assembly, candle-light glow, parade and calm corner.
   - Voice: `natasha-au`; original music and lyrics rather than relying on a stock recording.

10. **The Healthy-Habit Rhythm Relay**
    - User direction: good health habits for children.
    - Distinction from covered work: Bea’s broad five-location treasure trail already covers washing, teeth, food variety, movement and sleep. This new concept focuses on practising short routines through rhythm, sequencing and child-led demonstrations rather than revisiting Bea’s badge quest.
    - Structure: original action-song relay with sneeze/cough etiquette, water breaks, handwashing steps, screen/body breaks and asking a trusted grown-up for help when unwell.
    - Movement/visual system: split-screen child demonstrations, rhythmic pictograms and a routine timeline.
    - Voice: `ryan-uk`; original song and choreography.

11. **The Colour-Mixing March**
    - User direction: colour song.
    - Original song where three paint-drop characters combine in moving pairs to make orange, green and purple, then separate again. Use a marching-band visual system and real on-screen colour mixing rather than colour flashcards.
    - Voice: `ana-us`; brass-and-drum-inspired original arrangement.

12. **Breakfast Builders’ Morning Beat**
    - User direction: breakfast song.
    - Children assemble several culturally varied breakfast plates with help from grown-ups, practising sequence, food variety and listening to hunger/fullness cues without labelling foods “good” or “bad.”
    - Visual system: kitchen conveyor rhythm, ingredient motion and plate mosaics; original song.
    - Voice: `natasha-au`.
    - Prototype completed as the deliberately different `Nia's Rainbow Breakfast Dance`: an original 3D-look colour-and-movement chant using Nia plus red strawberry, yellow banana and blue blueberry friends. Preserve the broader culturally varied plate-building version as a possible later breakfast concept rather than duplicating this prototype.

13. **Dad’s Coming-Home Welcome Rhythm**
    - User direction: dad coming home song.
    - One specific family prepares three possible greetings—wave, hug if wanted, or a quiet drawing—then reconnects after Dad’s workday. Present this family warmly without implying every child has the same household.
    - Visual system: changing window light, clock movement, doorway reveal and family dance; original song.
    - Voice: `ryan-uk`, with a separate child dialogue voice.

14. **Mumma’s Shopping-Bag Story Surprise**
    - User direction: Mumma went shopping story.
    - A child stays safely with a known caregiver while Mum shops, predicts what reusable-bag shapes might hold, then helps sort pantry, fridge and household items when she returns. The story reinforces reunion and safe caregiving, not a child being left alone.
    - Visual system: home play sequence, illustrated thought bubbles, bag-unpacking categories and reunion.
    - Voice: `maisie-uk` narrator and `natasha-au` for Mum.

15. **Friends Build a Playground Song**
    - User direction: playing with friends song.
    - Children contribute different play ideas, ask before joining, handle “not yet,” and combine a chalk road, block town and movement game.
    - Visual system: overhead playground map that grows through collaboration; original ensemble song.
    - Voice: `ana-us` lead with varied short friend responses.

16. **The Toy-Sharing Repair Shop**
    - User direction: sharing toys.
    - A connected story—not a command to surrender possessions—in which children practise waiting, trading, setting a timer, playing together and keeping a special comfort toy private.
    - Visual system: toy workshop stations, timer animation and cooperative build payoff.
    - Voice: `natasha-au`.

17. **Eddie Excavator’s Careful Construction Day**
    - User direction: something around an excavator.
    - Eddie is a friendly excavator character who follows a plan to scoop, carry and level soil for a community rain garden while children stay behind a clear safety fence with a grown-up.
    - Visual system: side-view construction sequencing, moving arm/bucket joints, soil layers and a rain-garden transformation; not a vehicle quiz.
   - Voice: `ana-us` lead with `ryan-uk` as Eddie; rhythmic mechanical sound design.
   - Active on 2026-08-28 as `eddie-excavators-rain-garden-day-01`. The accepted premium miniature-diorama opening anchor is `automation/production-assets/eddie-excavator-rain-garden-opening-v1.png`; continue with distinct scoop, carry, level, safe-stop planting and water-test compositions.

18. **The Ice-Cream Melt-and-Mix Song**
    - User direction: ice cream song.
    - An original kitchen-science song about cold, melting, mixing flavours and sharing choices. Include dairy-free possibilities and grown-up preparation guidance without health claims.
    - Visual system: swirls, scoops, temperature pictograms and a melting-time visual experiment.
    - Voice: `maisie-uk`.

19. **Pick, Wash, Pack the Fruit Picnic**
    - User direction: picking up fruit song.
    - Children visit an orchard with permission and a grown-up, identify ripe fruit gently, wash it, count it and pack a colourful picnic without wasting produce.
    - Visual system: orchard rows, basket weight, washing-water motion and picnic pattern finale; original action song.
    - Voice: `natasha-au`.

20. **Animal Action Alphabet A-Z**
    - User direction: an educational idea like Animal Alphabet, with the user approving an action-based version and asking generation to resume.
    - Children meet one original 3D-look animal per letter and copy a memorable movement: Alligator amble, Bear bounce, Cat creep, Dog dance, Elephant stomp, Frog jump, Giraffe grow tall, Horse gallop, Iguana inch, Jaguar jog, Kangaroo jump, Lion leap, Monkey march, Narwhal nod, Owl open its wings, Penguin waddle, Quokka take quick steps, Rabbit reach, Seal sway, Tiger tiptoe, Urchin unfurl fingers, Vulture flap, Whale wave, X-ray tetra make an X, Yak yawn and Zebra zigzag.
    - Structure: a connected world-stage parade with short letter introductions and real movement windows, not a repeat of the existing static A-F or A-Z alphabet videos.
    - Visual system: original polished 3D-rendered animal cutouts, habitat-stage portals, tracking camera motion, action trails and large code-rendered letters. Do not copy any existing preschool brand, character style, song or set.
    - Voice: `maisie-uk`, following Nia's `ana-us` lead; use original rhythmic music and narration.
    - Completed on 2026-08-24 as `animal-action-alphabet-a-to-z-01` at 5:30 with 26 audited action windows, five original world stages and all 26 approved animal sprites.
