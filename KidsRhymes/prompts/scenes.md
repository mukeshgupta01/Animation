# Baa Baa Black Sheep — scene prompts

## Shared visual bible

Original 2D hand-painted storybook illustration; the same friendly fluffy black sheep with charcoal curls, warm cream muzzle, rounded ears, large kind brown eyes, and a small teal neck ribbon; warm golden farm light; clean expressive outlines; layered foreground/midground/background depth; rich flowers, rolling hills, wooden fences, barn, soft clouds, and painterly texture; calm preschool mood; cinematic 16:9 composition; generous safe margins around heads, ears, hats, and faces. No text, logo, watermark, copyrighted character, 3D rendering, primitive geometry, stick figures, photographic realism, or flat plain background.

## 01 — Flower meadow

Wide establishing shot: the black sheep stands in a lush flower meadow, smiling toward the viewer; buttercups and daisies in the foreground, winding fence and rolling green hills behind, red barn in the distance, blue sky with soft clouds, warm morning light; leave gentle motion space around the sheep for a slow push-in.

## 02 — Farmer and barn

Medium-wide shot: the same sheep beside an original kind farmer in blue overalls, cream shirt, straw hat, and brown boots near a warmly painted red barn; farmer kneels at a respectful distance and smiles at the sheep; wooden gate, hay, flowers, hills, and drifting clouds; all features fully framed.

## 03 — Three wool bags

Storybook farmyard scene: the same sheep proudly beside three plump cream canvas wool bags, one open just enough to show clean fluffy wool; barn wall, baskets, hollyhocks, fence, sunlit grass, layered foliage; clear readable silhouettes and room for a slow left-to-right camera move.

## 04 — Country lane

Wide country-lane scene: an original cheerful young child with curly dark hair, yellow sunhat, coral cardigan, teal shorts, and little boots walks safely down a flower-lined lane toward the farm; the same sheep watches from behind a low fence; cottage and hills in the distance, butterflies and soft clouds; calm, welcoming, fully framed composition.

## 05 — Sheep close-up

Intimate medium close-up of the same sheep in the meadow, ears and full head comfortably inside frame, expressive eyes and gentle smile, detailed charcoal wool curls and teal ribbon; foreground flowers softly overlap the lower edge, barn and hills softly layered behind; warm rim light and tiny restrained pollen sparkles.

## 06 — Sunny finale

Joyful wide finale: the same sheep, farmer, child, and a few original friendly farm animals gathered in the sunny flower meadow near the barn; three wool bags arranged naturally; everyone smiling, waving, or gently swaying; golden light, rolling hills, fence, drifting clouds, foreground flowers, celebratory but calm; ample safe margins and clean end-frame composition.
