OLLIE OTTER FLOATING PICNIC — ORIGINAL NARRATION

SCENE 1 — INVITATION
Sun on the river, picnic day!
Ollie smiles, Come this way!
Basket, blanket, water too—
How can three friends safely cruise?

SCENE 2 — LEFT HEAVY
Basket, blanket, jug go left.
Oops, that side has all the heft!
Low left and high right—
Keep it tied and keep it flat.

SCENE 3 — FIND BALANCE
Ollie rolls the blue jug right.
Willa watches, eyes so bright.
Left and right now share the load—
Level like a little road!

SCENE 4 — SECURE AND BOARD
Willa boards and sits down low.
Clip the straps before we go.
One friend moves, the others wait—
Patient turns will keep things straight.

SCENE 5 — CHORUS
Left and right, then find the middle,
Slow and steady, just a little.
Sit down low and help it glide,
Balance, balance, side to side!

SCENE 6 — MID-RIVER TILT
Fern sees ducks and waves hello.
One side dips—so soft and slow.
Point to centre, can you show?
Back we scoot before we row.

SCENE 7 — CENTRE AND PADDLE
Fern slides back—now centre, please.
Level raft and laughing breeze.
Paddles dip on either side—
Together, together, glide, glide, glide!

SCENE 8 — LAND AND UNLOAD
Ropes are tied at Picnic Bay.
Unload first—that is the way.
Hand to hand the basket goes,
Then the blanket softly rolls.

SCENE 9 — FINAL CHORUS
Left and right, then find the middle,
Slow and steady, just a little.
Friends who listen, friends who guide—
Balance, balance, side by side!
