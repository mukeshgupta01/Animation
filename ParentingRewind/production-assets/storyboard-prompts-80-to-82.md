# Storyboard prompts for episodes 80-82

Built-in image-generation mode was used for episodes 80 and 82. Episode 81 reuses the audited soccer storyboard with a new order and lesson.

## Episode 80

Photorealistic-natural adult-facing 3x2 storyboard: Southeast Asian-appearance father, four-year-old daughter and grandmother in a contemporary apartment. Beats: arrival, child declines hug, father pauses, offers non-contact choices, child and grandmother high-five, shared picture-book connection. Exactly six panels; consistent identities; no text, logos, forced touching, coercion or child-directed styling.

## Episode 82

Photorealistic-natural adult-facing 3x2 storyboard: Black mother and 16-year-old daughter with a blank achievement certificate. Beats: mother takes photo, begins to share while teen looks uncomfortable, pauses, asks permission, reviews choice together, keeps phone face-down and connects without posting. Exactly six panels; consistent identities; no readable text, logos, app UI or public-post implication.
