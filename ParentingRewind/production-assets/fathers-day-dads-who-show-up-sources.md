# Father's Day: dads who show up

This review-only Parenting Rewind tribute uses six existing project-owned
storyboard panels. It made zero new image-generation calls.

1. `bedtime-father-daughter-storyboard-01.png`, panel 4: reading and settling.
2. `laundry-father-two-children-storyboard-01.png`, panel 4: daily care and teamwork.
3. `soccer-father-son-storyboard-01.png`, panel 5: play, encouragement and connection.
4. `art-studio-father-daughter-storyboard-01.png`, panel 3: listening at child level.
5. `teen-curfew-father-daughter-storyboard-01.png`, panel 3: listening through adolescence.
6. `honesty-father-son-storyboard-01.png`, panel 5: repairing a mistake together.

The sequence deliberately rotates family appearance, setting, child age and
caregiving action. It does not represent one continuous family or imply that
fatherhood has one cultural or household form.

Research basis: American Academy of Pediatrics clinical report, *Fathers'
Roles in the Care and Development of Their Children: The Role of
Pediatricians*, reaffirmed April 2022.
