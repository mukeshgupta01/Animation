# Father's Day 20-second Shorts: image-generation record

Created 2026-09-03 with the built-in image-generation workflow. Five calls created five text-free, three-panel, native-portrait cinematic storyboards. No reference image was used. All prompts required independently crop-ready 9:16 panels, exact within-story character continuity, safe caption space, realistic hands and natural emotion without melodrama.

## 1. The Missed Call

Three-frame Indian-Australian father/daughter story: older father quietly hoping beside his phone and family photographs; busy adult daughter noticing missed calls; father's joyful video-call reaction. Warm amber and teal editorial family photography.

## 2. The Shoes by the Door

Three-frame East Asian-Australian father/daughter visual rhyme: young dad ties his little girl's school shoe; adult daughter later helps her elderly dad with his walking shoe; they walk hand-in-hand at golden hour. Warm wood, sage and amber palette.

## 3. His Old Wallet

Three-frame Black father/son story: worn leather wallet and faded childhood school photograph; adult son recognizes what Dad preserved; quiet kitchen hug. Tobacco brown, cream and muted blue palette.

## 4. One More Story

Three-frame Latino father/daughter story: young dad reads one more bedtime story; adult daughter returns years later; she reads beside her elderly father. Moonlit blue and warm amber palette.

## 5. The Empty Chair

Three-frame older father/family story: hopeful dad beside an empty dining chair; adult children arrive with a simple cake and flowers; the chair is filled as the family reconnects. Coral, warm gold and forest-green accents.

The full effective prompts also specified: one wide canvas with exactly three equal portrait panels arranged left-to-right, narrow clean gutters, no overlap, no text, no letters, no logos and no watermark.
