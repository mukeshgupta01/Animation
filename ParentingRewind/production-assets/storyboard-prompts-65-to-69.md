# Storyboard prompts for episodes 65-69

Generation method: built-in `image_gen`, one call per asset, no reference images.

Shared specification for all five prompts:

- Use case: `illustration-story`.
- Asset: strict six-panel 3x2 storyboard with equal panels and clean gutters, intended for portrait crops in an adult-facing parenting Short.
- Style: polished semi-realistic editorial lifestyle illustration with natural texture and emotionally readable acting; not childish or cartoonish.
- Character identities, ages, faces, hair and outfits must remain stable across all six panels.
- No readable text, speech bubbles, logos, watermarks, stereotypes, cultural costumes, national symbols, physical intimidation or glossy stock-photo styling.

## Episode 65: teen curfew

Cast and setting: South Asian-appearance father in his late 40s and 16-year-old daughter; contemporary apartment entry/dining area at night in navy and amber.

Panel beats: daughter arrives late while father notices clock; father points in frustration and daughter becomes defensive; father pauses to breathe; father listens at table; they make a practical phone/contact plan; daughter uses charging spot and father responds warmly.

Additional constraints: teenager must look 16; no punishment, surveillance or infantilizing.

## Episode 66: daycare separation

Cast and setting: dark-skinned mother in her mid-30s with short natural hair, 2.5-year-old son with small curls, and a supporting educator; bright contemporary early-learning entrance and cubbies in sage, yellow and timber.

Panel beats: toddler holds mother's leg; extended uncertain goodbye increases distress; mother pauses; short predictable goodbye with comfort toy; educator waits without grabbing during one wave; toddler begins settling safely after mother leaves.

Additional constraints: toddler must look 2.5; no forced smile, physical restraint, abandonment imagery or instant cheerful resolution.

## Episode 67: teen phone boundary

Cast and setting: Middle Eastern-appearance mother in her early 40s and 14-year-old son; townhouse dining/study area in plum, eucalyptus green and timber.

Panel beats: mother notices phone beside unfinished work; she reaches toward it and teen becomes defensive; mother pauses; mother listens; they use a neutral charging spot and clock; teen returns to work while mother gives space.

Additional constraints: teenager must look 14; no password demands, spying, confiscation or surveillance framing.

## Episode 68: toddler car-seat boundary

Cast and setting: East Asian-appearance father in his late 30s and 3-year-old daughter; stationary unbranded hatchback in an apartment parking area, sky blue, charcoal and coral.

Panel beats: daughter turns away from open rear door; rushed father points; father pauses safely; father offers climb-or-help choice; daughter climbs in with toy; daughter is correctly buckled while father reassures her.

Additional constraints: parked car only; no driving, physical force, restraint struggle or incorrect straps; final child-seat use must be correct.

## Episode 69: friendship listening

Cast and setting: grandfather in his early 60s with medium-brown skin, silver hair and glasses, and 11-year-old granddaughter with medium-brown skin and long dark curls; botanical garden cafe and path in green, rust and cream.

Panel beats: granddaughter is upset by an unbranded phone message; grandfather gives too much advice; grandfather pauses; he listens while she explains; he offers listening-or-ideas choice; they walk together without implying the friendship is magically fixed.

Additional constraints: child must look 11; no phone spying, exaggerated sadness or instant happy ending.
