## Episode 83: preschool friendship conflict

Create six panels in a leafy neighborhood park: the same warm-tan-skinned aunt in her late 30s and five-year-old nephew support a chalk-sharing disagreement with another five-year-old. Show the conflict, the aunt beginning to take over, her pause, coaching feeling words, the child trying those words, and the children drawing connected shapes while the aunt steps back.

## Episode 85: teen phone and sleep plan
