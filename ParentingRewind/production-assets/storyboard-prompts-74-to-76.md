# Storyboard prompts for episodes 74-76

Created: 2026-08-26

The asset library and all 99 indexed topics were audited before generation. Two new storyboards were required because existing panels could not accurately communicate toddler biting or a bullying disclosure. Episode 76 deliberately reuses the existing teen-grade family in a different panel sequence and for a distinct stress-and-schedule lesson.

## Episode 74: toddler biting

Built-in image generation, one successful call. A polished photorealistic 3x2 storyboard showing a fictional Indigenous Australian-appearance father and his 2.5-year-old daughter in a modern playgroup: toy conflict, safe non-graphic aftermath, parent pause, calm limit, modeling simple words and gentle hands, and supported repair. The prompt prohibited injury, shown biting, text, logos, party décor, shaming and inconsistent casting.

Final asset: `production-assets/toddler-biting-father-daughter-storyboard-01.png`

## Episode 75: bullying disclosure

Built-in image generation, one successful call. A polished photorealistic 3x2 storyboard showing a fictional white Australian mother and her ten-year-old son: after-school withdrawal, an alarmed fixing reaction, parent pause, listening, private note-taking and walking toward the school office together. The prompt prohibited a visible aggressor, violence, readable writing, branding, melodrama and a magically solved ending.

Final asset: `production-assets/bullying-disclosure-mother-son-storyboard-01.png`

## Episode 76: teen overload

No new production asset was generated. Two attempts to create a teen-sports storyboard were rejected by the built-in image safety system and were not saved or used. The episode instead reuses `production-assets/teen-grade-mother-daughter-storyboard-01.png` with a distinct lesson, narration and panel order.
