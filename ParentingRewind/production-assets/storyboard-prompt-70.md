# Storyboard prompt for episode 70

Generation method: built-in `image_gen`, one call, no reference images.

Use case: a six-panel adult-facing parenting education storyboard for a vertical short. The requested and accepted style is premium cinematic photorealistic editorial lifestyle photography with natural skin texture, realistic warm kitchen lighting, and no text, logos, watermarks, speech bubbles, illustration or cartoon styling.

Identity lock: the same mother in her early forties with medium-brown skin, dark braids, cream knit sweater and teal trousers, and the same fifteen-year-old daughter with medium-brown skin, shoulder-length dark hair, green school cardigan and white shirt appear in all six panels.

Panel beats:

1. The teenager places a disappointing returned test on the kitchen table while her mother notices.
2. The mother reacts too quickly with a worried, stern posture; the teenager looks away and folds inward.
3. The mother steps back, lowers her hands and takes one calming breath.
4. The mother sits beside the teenager with open hands and listens while she explains what felt hard.
5. They review a notebook and blank sticky notes to choose one realistic study step.
6. The notebook is closed, both faces soften, and connection remains without an exaggerated celebration.

The prompt required a different camera angle, composition, posture and action in every panel and prohibited pointing, repetitive folded arms, readable grades, malformed hands, duplicated objects, shame, dramatic crying and a magically perfect ending.

Visual review: accepted. The cast, clothing and room are consistent; the six emotional/action beats are distinct; faces and visible hands are natural; the final panel communicates reconnection without overstating the outcome.
