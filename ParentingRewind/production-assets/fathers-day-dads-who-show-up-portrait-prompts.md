# Father's Day portrait visual prompts

Created 2026-09-02 with six separate calls to the built-in image-generation tool. All assets use the `photorealistic-natural` taxonomy and were generated natively for a 9:16 adult-facing Parenting Rewind Short. Shared constraints: keep full faces, hands and the emotional action inside the central 65%; reserve uncluttered space at the top for chapter labels and the lower 25% for captions; no text, logo, watermark, collage, cropped heads, cartoon styling or purple-dominated palette.

1. `fathers-day-quiet-love-portrait-01.png`: an exhausted but tender father reading one more bedtime story to his young daughter, warm bedside light and quiet intimacy.
2. `fathers-day-daily-work-portrait-01.png`: a father packing school lunch and folding a uniform while two children get ready, warm early-morning household care.
3. `fathers-day-repair-portrait-01.png`: a father kneeling at his school-age son's eye level to repair after a hard moment, with a tipped wooden toy as a restrained story cue.
4. `fathers-day-listening-child-portrait-01.png`: an attentive father listening to his school-age daughter after a hard school day in the entryway, backpack visible.
5. `fathers-day-listening-teen-portrait-01.png`: a slightly older father listening patiently to his teenage son at the kitchen table in warm evening light.
6. `fathers-day-family-hug-portrait-01.png`: a multigenerational family embracing an older father figure at golden hour, genuine spontaneous warmth.

These are six independent project-owned generated images, not crops from a landscape storyboard. They may be reused sparingly for fatherhood, responsive-care, repair, listening and appreciation topics when the scene remains accurate.
