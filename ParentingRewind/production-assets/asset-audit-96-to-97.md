# Asset and topic audit: episodes 96–97

Reviewed 22 September 2026. User requested exactly two new parenting videos.

The topic index and metadata through episode 95 were checked before scripting.
96 teaches joining and imitating a preschooler's safe self-directed play. Earlier
episodes used praise for chores and cooperation, or discussed picture books;
none showed this block-and-truck play lesson. The existing reading-corner and
screen-time families do not provide the necessary imitation and block-play
beats. One new built-in storyboard was generated.

97 focuses on the parent's own phone distraction during a teen conversation.
It differs from episode 82 (permission before posting a teen photo), 85 (overnight
phone and sleep plan), and 89 (an alarmed response to a disclosure). The existing
mother/teen dining-room panels accurately show distraction, pausing, discussion
and phone-down attention. Reuse order 1,2,5,3,5,3 omits the photo-taking panel and
does not repeat episode 82's full sequence. No new image generation for 97.

Both videos preserve full panels over a soft portrait background, retaining both
faces and relevant hands. Narration, labels, title, captions and descriptions are
new. The stories show different ages, caregivers and family casts. Local draft
production does not increment published-use counters.

  https://www.cdc.gov/parenting-toddlers/communication/index.html
  Supports imitating a child's play and describing their actions. The older
  special-playtime URL did not return usable content, so it is not used as the
  source. The few-minutes example is practical wording, not a validated dosage.
- AAP, Be a Digital Role Model for Your Child, reviewed 22 September 2026:
  https://www.healthychildren.org/English/family-life/Media/Pages/be-a-digital-role-model.aspx
  Supports reviewing adult habits, being present with family and making time for
  activities without screens. Suggested dialogue is original; no guaranteed
  improvement or personalised clinical advice is claimed.
